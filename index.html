AUSTRALIAN CREATIVE INDUSTRIES / DATA STORY

The Shape of Creativity

Exploring the economic value of Australia's creative industries and how Australians engage with the arts.

01 / The Creative Economy

Economic values are reported at current prices and are not adjusted for inflation.

Cultural and creative GDP increased from A$47.8 billion in 2015–16 to A$70.3 billion in 2024–25.

However, its share of Australia's total GDP fell from 2.87% to 2.53% over the same period.

Specialised activity made up the largest share of cultural and creative gross value added (GVA) throughout the period.

Support and embedded activities both increased in value, but their shares were lower in 2024–25 than in 2015–16.
